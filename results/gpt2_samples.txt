ROMEO:
I cannot mock thee, for thou art not:
If thou wilt pity be taken: be gone, thou,
And I'll have thy shame before my shame.
Didst thank thee, lady, Lord Northumberland?

RATCLIFF:
My gracious silence know his good prayers;
And from his mouth he hath murdered there not in me;
But none else I will not name myself die.

KING RICHARD II:
What is

-----

ROMEO:
This is, and all the worse.

JULIET:
O God!

ROMEO:
Is love--

JULIET:
I do remember it so; and the better is more words--
As living here I will be a fool--to?
Or here, by my soul's love, I love,
Go to, away with me; alack the earth,
And thou comest ere once dream.



-----

ROMEO:
Good business for me;
How many of all my heart
